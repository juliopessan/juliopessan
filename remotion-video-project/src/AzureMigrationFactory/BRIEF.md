# The Azure Migration Factory: 30-Second Executive Cut

Roteiro/brief criativo original, preservado na íntegra para orientar a geração
dos clipes (ex: Runway, Sora, Veo, Kling) e a produção de trilha/VO. A
composição Remotion em `Main.tsx` monta esses elementos com o timing exato
abaixo — veja `data.ts` para os frames e `README.md` para como plugar os assets.

## Estética geral (base para todos os prompts)

Premium photorealistic footage, ARRI Alexa 35 texture, high-end corporate
tech-commercial lighting, deep navy and Azure-blue palette with subtle
ember-orange accents. Sharp art direction, subtle natural grain, high dynamic
range.

## Ato 1: O Gancho (0:00–0:05) — O Risco do Status Quo

**VO:** "Neste exato momento, nossa maior barreira para o crescimento não é o
mercado. É a nossa própria fundação."

**Câmera:** OPENING SHOT — 107° wide rectilinear view. The camera sits only
60 cm above a glossy dark glass table. A glowing holographic legacy
datacenter model looms large in the immediate foreground. The lead architect
(sharp navy suit, ember-orange pocket square) leans toward the lens from the
midground, looking serious, and points directly at the tangled legacy data
streams. A rapid physical tabletop push-in ends with the holographic data
passing close above the lens.

## Ato 2: A Lentidão Manual (0:05–0:12) — O Problema Real

**VO:** "Entender sistemas legados de forma manual consome meses. E migrações
baseadas em suposições estouram prazos e orçamentos."

**Câmera:** WHIP CUT to MATCH CUT — A glowing blue digital portal fills the
frame. The camera bursts through it into a surreal modern data center. An
architect passes a heavy, dark legacy physical hard drive through a round
opening in a server rack. The drive crosses the lens and emerges into the
hands of another architect in a polished, futuristic cloud control room. The
physical drive instantly transforms into a glowing, complex Azure data
hologram. The camera performs a fast 180-degree orbit around her as she
swipes the messy hologram apart.

## Ato 3: A Engenharia Determinística (0:12–0:18) — O Ponto de Virada

**VO:** "Mudamos a abordagem. Mapeamos a infraestrutura de forma
determinística e desenhamos o futuro no Azure antes de construí-lo."

**Câmera:** OVERHEAD SHOT & SENSORY INSERTS — Perfect top-down view of four
architects standing in a clean radial composition around a giant circular
glowing architecture table. They pass digital workload packets clockwise.
Controlled rapid macro cuts (18° detail optics): fiber optic cables
illuminating with intense blue light; the exact geometric edges of a pristine
Azure cloud node forming; fingertips typing rapidly on a transparent glass
interface, replacing chaotic legacy code with perfect geometric Azure
structures.

## Ato 4: O Valor de Negócio (0:18–0:25) — O Ecossistema Azure

**VO:** "O resultado? Governança de ponta a ponta, otimização exata de custos
e a plataforma perfeita para escalar Inteligência Artificial."

**Câmera:** LOW-ANGLE HERO SHOT — 107° wide rectilinear view from floor
height. The four architects form a loose diamond around the central pristine
Azure cloud pedestal. The lead steps across the lens, then the camera cranes
rapidly upward between them as they turn and look directly into the camera.
Coats and fabrics react naturally with delayed physical motion. Their
expressions shift from intense analytical focus into confident, successful
smiles.

## Ato 5: Call to Action (0:25–0:30) — O Fechamento

**VO:** "Não é apenas uma mudança de data center. É velocidade de mercado.
Vamos migrar."

**Câmera:** FINAL GROUP SHOT to PACKSHOT — 84° wide view. The camera makes a
fast curved dolly move around the group. Each architect holds a glowing
holographic tablet, they bring the tablets toward the center for a
synchronized swipe deployment. HARD CUT to a pristine deep dark-blue studio
background. One exact Azure Migration architectural node stands upright on a
mirror-polished pedestal. A slow 29° short-telephoto push creates subtle
premium parallax while the glowing architecture stays razor-sharp.

## Notas de direção (áudio e ritmo)

**Trilha sonora:** nos Atos 1 e 2, baixo tenso e denso (sintetizadores
pesados). No corte do Ato 3 (Overhead Shot), a trilha "abre", tornando-se
limpa, inspiradora e rítmica, culminando com o som de "ativação digital"
(chime) no Ato 5 durante o movimento sincronizado dos tablets.
